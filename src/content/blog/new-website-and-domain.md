Hello, Crystal community!

I'm excited to share that Ameba finally has a proper home on the web — a dedicated website and domain.

Up until now, our online presence has mostly been centered around the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/crystal-ameba/ameba). While that's still where all the development happens, we felt it was time to give Ameba a more accessible and user-friendly face.

## Welcome to [crystal-ameba.org](https://crystal-ameba.org) — the new official website for Ameba.

First of all, a big thank you to [@veelenga](https://github.com/veelenga) for creating the new website! ❤️

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and is designed to be a central hub for documentation, guides, and announcements. Here's what you can expect:

- **Easier onboarding** — A streamlined getting-started guide for new users.
- **Blog** — A place for project news, releases, and community updates (you're reading it!).
- **Clearer documentation** — In the future, organized rule references and configuration guides will be available as well.
- **Open source** — The website itself is [open on GitHub](https://github.com/crystal-ameba/crystal-ameba.github.io), so contributions are welcome.

This move also comes with a new domain — `crystal-ameba.org` — which we hope will be easier to remember and share.

API documentation URLs are now versioned (`crystal-ameba.org/api/<version>`) and have changed the address from `crystal-ameba.github.io/ameba` to `crystal-ameba.org/api/master`.

If you have any feedback or ideas for the website, feel free to [open an issue](https://github.com/crystal-ameba/crystal-ameba.github.io/issues/new) or reach out on the [Crystal community channels](https://crystal-lang.org/community).
